# ESIP IT&I Committee

This is a read-only archive of the ESIP Information Technology & Interoperability (IT&I) Committee's pages, migrated from the ESIP MediaWiki to keep them durable and readable.

- **Current committee activities:** [ESIP IT&I committee page](https://www.esipfed.org/get-involved/collaborate/iti)

- **Live ESIP wiki:** [wiki.esipfed.org](https://wiki.esipfed.org/)

Only the committee's own content is re-hosted here. Pages it merely linked to — other ESIP clusters and federation-wide material — are not archived and point back to the live wiki.
