# Meetings

The committee meets on the second Thursday of each month at 3 PM Eastern. See the
[ESIP community calendar](https://www.esipfed.org/get-involved/community-calendar)
for connection details.

## 2026-11-12

A brainstorm for the January 2027 ESIP Winter Meeting. Participants introduced
themselves with something exciting and something frustrating in IT interoperability.
The discussion settled on an exercise: participants each take an achievable but
non-trivial cross-agency data question, spend about an hour on it with current data
systems and AI tools, and report what was and was not possible in lightning talks,
with plans and results kept on GitHub.

## 2026-10-15

A demo of the IT&I wiki's migration to this GitHub Pages site, followed by discussion
of how the community should use the repository and what topics the committee should
take up next. Four ideas were recorded for follow-up as
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/ESIPFed/itandi-committee/issues).

## 2026-09-10

The first meeting with returning chair David Blodgett, organized around three
questions: how people prefer to learn what's new in Earth science IT, what would
motivate them to contribute, and what gap the committee could fill. Answers favored
short talks with linked background material, demonstrations and collaborative
testing, and a GitHub site whose issues feed a Slack channel.
